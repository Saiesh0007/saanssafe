      How it decides
      
    
      
      
      01Live forecast
      Hourly PM2.5, PM10, temperature and humidity for your exact location (Open-Meteo / Copernicus CAMS).
      
      
      02Indian AQI
      CPCB breakpoints, not the US scale, so numbers match Indian news and SAFAR.
      
      
      03School hours only
      Each activity is judged on its own window: assembly 7-9, recess 10-12, PE 9-14, dismissal 13-15.
      
      
      04Clear verdicts
      Go · Go with care · Limit · Move indoors · Stay home. PE steps up a level; asthma mode is stricter.
      
    
      
    
      Guidance tool, not a medical or government order. Always follow your state's DoE / GRAP circulars.
