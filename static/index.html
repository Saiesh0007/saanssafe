      How it decides
      
    
        
      
      1. Live forecast - hourly PM2.5, PM10, temperature and humidity for your exact location (Open-Meteo / Copernicus CAMS).
        2. 
      
      2. Indian AQI - computed with CPCB breakpoints, not the US scale, so numbers match what Indian news and SAFAR report.
        3. 
      
      3. School hours only - every activity is judged on its own time window (assembly 7-9, recess 10-12, PE 9-14, dismissal 13-15).
        4. 
      
      4. Clear verdicts - Go · Go with care · Limit · Move indoors · Stay home. Strenuous PE steps up one level; asthma mode is stricter.
        5. 
    
      
    
      Guidance tool, not a medical or government order. Always follow your state's DoE / GRAP circulars.
